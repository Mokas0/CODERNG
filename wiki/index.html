Game Overview

CODERNG is a browser-based RNG aura rolling game. World 1 has the full roll pool and no luck cap; World 2 is a separate page with 20 ultra-rare auras and a 30× effective luck cap. The game features multiple shops and NPCs (regular shop, Benny, Patrick, Sneho, Theo, Jia in World 2, the Crafter, and the Material Seller), a luck system with potions and gear, crafting, global biome events, hidden Elder and Secret auras, the Supreme King and Void Queen, a casino, a player marketplace, quests, and a tycoon clicker.

Everything runs in the browser with Supabase powering multiplayer features.
