🌌 Cosmology: In The Beginning

The foundational lore of the CODERNG universe — a tragedy baked into its bones.

The Infinite

Before all things, there was the Infinite — a conscious, boundless being of pure creative will. The Infinite did not stumble into existence; it chose it. From itself, it shaped two realms: the Heavens, a domain of light, order, and ascension, and the Hells, a kingdom of shadow, depth, and entropy. These were not good and evil — they were two halves of a whole, meant to exist in eternal tension and eternal harmony.

Having built the stage, the Infinite appointed two rulers and withdrew. Whether it watches still, or has moved on to create other things entirely, no one — not even the Supreme King himself — knows for certain.

The Supreme King & The Void Queen

The Infinite chose two beings to govern what it had made. The Supreme King was given dominion over the Heavens — radiant, commanding, the embodiment of sovereignty and light. The Void Queen was given dominion over the Hells — vast, enigmatic, the embodiment of depth and the spaces between things.

They were not enemies. Once, they were something far more dangerous: they were everything to each other.

What broke between them is a matter of myth and argument. Some say the King reached too far into the Hells. Some say the Queen refused to let the Heavens touch her domain. Whatever the wound was, it did not heal — and a universe governed by two rulers who no longer speak except through intermediaries is a universe quietly coming apart at the seams.

The Council of Five Emperors

Before the fracture fully set in, the King and Queen created together — their last true act of collaboration. They shaped five Emperors, cosmic beings each embodying a fundamental pillar of existence. The Emperors answer to both rulers in theory, and to neither in practice. They are the closest thing the universe has to neutral ground.

The five Emperors and their domains are to be developed — consider tying each to a theme: creation, destruction, time, soul, and change.

The Council of Ten — Ascendants & Elders

Beneath the Emperors, the King and Queen each contributed to a second council: ten beings known as the Ascendants and Elders. The Ascendants trend toward the Heavens; the Elders lean toward the Hells. They govern the finer machinery of the universe — law, death, seasons, war, memory — and they are the beings most likely to actually interact with the mortal world.

The Doorways & The Coming of Humans

The broken bond between the King and Queen did not just wound them. It wounded the universe. Over centuries, the imbalance between Heaven and Hell caused the fabric between realms to tear — not catastrophically, but quietly, like cracks spreading through old stone.

Through these tears, humans entered the world. Not summoned. Not created. Not welcome, exactly — just there, suddenly, blinking in a world built for beings far greater than themselves. They were an accident of cosmic grief.

Humans had no divine mandate, no inherent power, no seat at any council. But the world they'd fallen into was soaked in cosmic energy — and over generations, something began to stir within them. Slowly, quietly, auras emerged.

In-Game Connections

This cosmology underpins the game's aura hierarchy: Elders, Ascendants, Emperors, the Supreme King, and the Void Queen are not arbitrary rarities — they are echoes of this cosmic order.

Natural next steps for the bible:
  • Define the five Emperors and their domains
  • Name and role the ten Ascendants/Elders — these are the closest things to "active gods" in human affairs
  • Establish what humans know vs. what is myth — does the average person know about the King and Queen's broken bond, or is that sacred/forbidden knowledge?
  • Tie aura types back to this hierarchy — do certain auras echo the Emperors? Can a human with the right aura challenge the cosmic order?
