Elder, Ascendant & Emperor Auras

The rarest auras in the game, unlocked through secret conditions — from Elders to the ultimate Emperors.

How It Works

  1. Meet the secret condition(s) — checked automatically as you play
  2. The aura is "unlocked" and enters the roll pool at 9Q weight (1/9,000,000,000,000,000)
  3. You must then actually ROLL it — unlocking doesn't grant it
  4. Once rolled, it's removed from the pool (you can only get each one once)
  5. Rolling triggers an unskippable staged cutscene

Pro Tip

Ascendants are combinations of Elder conditions. If you've unlocked all 5 Elders, you're already close to many Ascendant requirements.

Final Boss

THE INFINITE requires all 4 other Emperors to be received first. It is the ultimate endgame trophy — the last aura you will ever unlock.

Display Rarity

All Elder, Ascendant, and Emperor auras show 9Q (9,000,000,000,000,000) rarity. This is for display only — once unlocked, their roll weight is 1/9Q which makes them extremely rare even in the pool.

THE SUPREME KING — ♔ UNOBTAINABLE ♔

Above even the Emperors exists a single aura that cannot be unlocked through any gameplay condition or rolled from the pool.

ID Name Tier How to Obtain
9999 ♔ THE SUPREME KING ♔ UNOBTAINABLE Use the Supreme Luck Potion (1/1,000 per use)
  • The Supreme Luck Potion is sold exclusively by Benny with only a 1/100 chance to appear per visit
  • Each use has a flat 1/1,000 chance to summon THE SUPREME KING — player luck does NOT affect this
  • Triggers an unskippable ~15-second cutscene with 6 dramatic stages
  • Displayed in gold (#ffd700) Cinzel font with multi-layered fire/gold text shadows
  • History shows with a golden-to-fire pulsing gradient and "♔ UNOBTAINABLE" badge
Beyond the Emperors

THE SUPREME KING is the only UNOBTAINABLE-tier aura. Unlike Elders, Ascendants, and Emperors — which are unlocked by meeting conditions and then rolled — THE SUPREME KING bypasses the roll pool entirely and is summoned directly by the Supreme Luck Potion.

THE VOID QUEEN (World 2)

The enemy of the Supreme King. Obtainable only in World 2 by using the Potion of Destruction — a crafted potion made at the Crafter from 5× each of four materials bought from Jia (NULL FRAGMENT, VOID SHARD, THE UNSPOKEN, ABSOLUTE ZERO — 20 items total, 8,150+ NULL COINS). Extremely hard to obtain. Using the potion guarantees the Void Queen aura and triggers an extended cutscene. Not rollable.

Jia Void Auras (World 2 — Void Potion)

Buying the Void Potion from Jia (1,200 NULL COINS) grants one of three Supreme King–level auras at random, each with its own extended cutscene:

  • ♔ VOID SOVEREIGN ♔
  • ♔ THE NULL EMPEROR ♔
  • ♔ ETERNAL NULL ♔

These are not in the roll pool; the Void Potion is the only source.

BOOK OF POWER (World 2)

Obtainable only from a Void Potion in World 2, and only after you have already received both Void Queen and THE SUPREME KING. When those conditions are met, each Void Potion use has a 1/500 chance to grant BOOK OF POWER instead of one of the three usual Jia Void auras. The cutscene features particles, special effects, and the text: "Well, you found me. After all of that suffering, how do you feel? Great, its time for your REAL journey to begin."
