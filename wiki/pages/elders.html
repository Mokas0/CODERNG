Elder & Ascendant Auras

The rarest auras in the game, unlocked through secret conditions.

How It Works

  1. Meet the secret condition(s) — checked automatically as you play
  2. The aura is "unlocked" and enters the roll pool at 9Q weight (1/9,000,000,000,000,000)
  3. You must then actually ROLL it — unlocking doesn't grant it
  4. Once rolled, it's removed from the pool (you can only get each one once)
  5. Rolling triggers an unskippable staged cutscene

Elder Auras

5 total, each requires ONE condition

ID Name Condition
9950 THE GLUTTON Buy 1,000 items from Sneho's shop
9951 THE HOARDER Accumulate 500 scraps
9952 THE ANCIENT Perform 10,000 rolls
9953 THE FORSAKEN Get cursed 100 times (from Sneho items)
9954 THE DEVOTED Spend 1,000,000 coins total

Ascendant Auras

5 total, each requires TWO simultaneous conditions

ID Name Condition 1 Condition 2
9960 THE PRIMORDIAL 10,000 rolls 500 scraps
9961 THE CONDEMNED 100 curses 1,000 Sneho purchases
9962 THE ABSOLUTE 1,000,000 coins spent 10,000 rolls
9963 THE RELENTLESS 1,000 Sneho purchases 1,000,000 coins spent
9964 THE OMNISCIENT 100 curses 500 scraps
Pro Tip

Ascendants are combinations of Elder conditions. If you've unlocked all 5 Elders, you're already close to many Ascendant requirements.

Display Rarity

All Elder and Ascendant auras show 9Q (9,000,000,000,000,000) rarity. This is for display only — once unlocked, their roll weight is 1/9Q which makes them extremely rare even in the pool.
