Bazaar

The player-to-player aura marketplace.

Requirements

  • Requires sign-in (email/password authentication via Supabase)
  • Separate from the Casino username system
  • Must link your Casino vault using a link code to transfer auras

Coin Wallet

  • The Bazaar has its own coin wallet (separate from local coins and Casino wallet)
  • Deposit coins from your local balance to the Bazaar wallet
  • Withdraw Bazaar coins back to local balance
  • All trades use Bazaar coins

Selling Auras

  • Import auras from your linked Casino vault into Bazaar inventory
  • Set a price in coins and list the aura for sale
  • Other players can browse and buy your listed auras
  • Cancel a listing at any time to return the aura to inventory

Buying Auras

  • Browse auras listed by other players
  • Purchase with Bazaar coins
  • Bought auras go to your Bazaar inventory
  • Transfer to Casino vault, then withdraw to local storage

Stocks — BZX

  • The Bazaar Index (BZX) is a stock whose price reflects marketplace activity
  • Price rises with sales volume and trade activity over the last 24 hours
  • Buy shares with Bazaar coins; sell to get coins back
  • Track your holdings and P&L in the Stocks panel

Investing in Sellers

  • Invest Bazaar coins in other players' businesses
  • Sellers with active listings appear in the Invest section
  • Divest at any time to withdraw your investment back to your Bazaar wallet
  • Track your investments in the "My investments" section

Flow Overview

Flow Summary

To sell an aura: Local Storage → Casino Vault → Bazaar Inventory → List for Sale.

To buy and use: Purchase → Bazaar Inventory → Casino Vault → Local Storage.
