Tycoon

A clicker minigame for earning coins.

How It Works

  • Click the button to earn coins (Coins Per Click = CPC)
  • Starting CPC: 1
  • Buy upgrades with coins to increase CPC
  • Each upgrade adds +10 CPC
  • Maximum 50 upgrades

Upgrade Costs

Formula: cost = 500 × 1.5upgradeIndex

Cost scales exponentially but not too steeply (1.5× per level).

Stats Tracked

  • Total clicks
  • Total coins earned from tycoon
  • Current CPC
  • Current upgrade level
Max Upgrades

At max upgrades (50), CPC = 501. Each click earns 501 coins, making the Tycoon a reliable income source alongside salvaging.
