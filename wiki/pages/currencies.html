Currencies

All the economies in CODERNG.

Coins

Main currency. Used for: potions, luck boosts, Sneho items, Tycoon upgrades, Casino stakes, Bazaar trades.

Sources

  • Salvaging items from Past Rolls
  • Tycoon clicks
  • Quest rewards (500K–50M per quest)
  • Casino/Bazaar withdrawals
  • PayPal store purchases
  • Admin grants (testing)

Salvage Formula

coinsForSalvage(rarity) = min(10000, max(1, floor(100 / log10(rarity + 1))))

Salvage Tip

Lower rarity items actually give more coins when salvaged! Common items are the best coin source from salvaging.

Scraps

Secondary currency earned from salvaging. Used to buy permanent gear from Theo's Workshop.

Scrap Strategy

Rarer items have higher scrap drop rates AND give more scraps. Save your rare rolls for scrapping if you need gears.

Gear Bonus

Permanent luck bonus from Theo's gears. Adds to your total luck on every roll (doesn't reset). All 5 gears combined = +402 permanent luck. See the Shops page for the full gear list.

Luck

Not a currency per se, but a core resource.

Sources

  • Potions (temporary, resets after each roll)
  • Gear bonus (permanent)
  • Buy Luck button: +5 luck for 50 × currentMult coins (gets expensive fast)
  • Memory game: +10 luck on completion
  • PayPal store luck boosts
