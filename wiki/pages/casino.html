Casino

Wager coins or auras against other players.

Getting Started

  • The Casino requires a username (shared with Hub). Set one in the Casino tab.
  • Your Casino wallet is separate from your main coins — deposit to play, withdraw to take winnings.
  • The Casino uses Supabase for real-time multiplayer.

Coinflip

  • Create a challenge: pick an amount and a side (Heads or Tails)
  • Another player accepts and matches the stake
  • A random flip decides the winner — winner takes the full pot
  • 50/50 odds, pure luck

Itemflip

  • Create a challenge: stake an aura from your Casino vault
  • Another player accepts with an aura of equal or higher rarity
  • Random winner takes both auras
  • Great for gambling rare auras

Aura Vault

  • Deposit auras from your Past Rolls or Locked Storage into the Casino vault
  • Withdraw auras from the vault back to your local storage
  • Vault is stored server-side (Supabase) — persists across devices if linked

Link Code System

  • Generate a 6-character link code in the Casino
  • Use the code in the Bazaar to link your Casino vault to your Bazaar account
  • Codes expire after ~10 minutes
  • Allows transferring auras between Casino and Bazaar
