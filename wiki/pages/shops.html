All Shops

Five different shops, each with unique mechanics.

Regular Potion Shop

  • Rotation: every 5 minutes
  • Offers 3–4 random potions from the base pool
  • Prices randomly discounted 85–100% of base
  • 0.8% chance per rotation to include the Legendary Luck Elixir
  • Potions go to your inventory (use when ready)

Benny the Wanderer

  • Appears randomly within each 60-minute window
  • No warning — you have to notice the popup
  • Sells all regular potions at 90% cost
  • Has 5 exclusive potions not sold anywhere else:
Name Cost Luck Bonus

👑✨ Supreme Luck Potion 150,000 +30,000,000

Best luck-per-coin value in the game. The Supreme Luck Potion is exceptionally rare — it has only a 1-in-100 chance to appear each time Benny visits. It can also trigger THE SUPREME KING aura (1/10,000 per use).

Sneho's Forbidden Wares

Rotation: every 10 minutes. Offers 3 random items from a 7-item pool. GAMBLE: each item has a cursed chance — if cursed, you get negative luck instead. Effect applies immediately (not inventoried).
