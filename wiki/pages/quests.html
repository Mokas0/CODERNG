Quest Board

Complete daily and weekly missions for massive coin rewards.

Daily Quests

  • 3 random quests from a pool of 12
  • Reset every 24 hours
  • Seeded shuffle ensures same quests per period for all players

Weekly Quests

  • 2 random quests from a pool of 8
  • Reset every 7 days
  • Higher rewards than daily quests

Quest Types

Type Tracking Description
roll Cumulative Count total rolls
salvage Cumulative Count salvaged items
sneho_buy Cumulative Count Sneho purchases
sneho_curse Cumulative Count curses received from Sneho
shop_spend Cumulative Total coins spent in shops
earn_scraps Cumulative Total scraps earned
potion_buy Cumulative Count potions purchased
luck_reach Peak value Reach a specific luck multiplier
rarity_hit Peak value Roll an item of a specific rarity or higher
Peak-Value Quests

Peak-value quests (luck_reach, rarity_hit) track your best single value, not cumulative totals. Rolling a 1B rarity item once is enough — you don't need to do it multiple times.
