Potions

Buy potions to stash them in your inventory, then use them before a big roll.

How the Inventory Works

  • Buying a potion adds it to your Potion Stash
  • Potions stay in the stash until you choose to use them
  • "Use 1" applies a single potion's luck bonus
  • "Use All" dumps your entire stash into luck at once
  • Luck resets to 1× after you roll, so use potions right before rolling

Strategy Tips

  • Buy cheap potions across multiple shop rotations and stockpile
  • Use All before your next roll for maximum luck
  • Benny's Bargain Brew is the best luck-per-coin deal at 3.75 luck/coin
  • The Legendary Elixir is rare but very powerful when it appears
