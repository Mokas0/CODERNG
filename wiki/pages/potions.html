Potions

Buy potions to stash them in your inventory, then use them before a big roll.

How the Inventory Works

  • Buying a potion adds it to your Potion Stash
  • Potions stay in the stash until you choose to use them
  • "Use 1" applies a single potion's luck bonus
  • "Use All" dumps your entire stash into luck at once
  • Luck resets to 1× after you roll, so use potions right before rolling

Supreme Luck Potion — Extremely Rare

The Supreme Luck Potion has only a 1-in-100 chance to appear each time Benny visits. When used, it has a 1-in-1,000 chance to summon THE SUPREME KING — an UNOBTAINABLE-tier aura. Your luck does NOT affect the Supreme King spawn chance.

Secret Aura Chance

The Ultraluck Potion has a 1-in-5,000,000 chance to trigger a Secret Aura per use. Multiple Ultraluck potions stack: when you use N Ultraluck potions at once (e.g. "Use All"), the chance becomes 1 − (1 − 1/5,000,000)^N — so 10 potions give roughly 10× the chance, 100 potions give roughly 100×, etc.

Incarnatus & Geometrical Auras

The Incarnatus potion is an ultra-rare Sneho offer (1/5000 chance per rotation). It costs 25,000 coins and grants a random Geometrical aura — one of 10 auras based on the top 10 Geometry Dash pointercrate levels. Geometrical auras are not in the normal roll pool; Incarnatus is the only way to obtain them. Luck does not affect the Incarnatus spawn chance.

World 2 Exclusive Potions

In World 2 (separate page), two special potions exist that do not grant luck — they grant auras directly.

  • Void Potion — Bought from Jia for 1,200 NULL COINS. Grants one of three Supreme King–level auras at random (VOID SOVEREIGN, THE NULL EMPEROR, ETERNAL NULL), each with an extended cutscene. If you already have both Void Queen and Supreme King, there is a 1/500 chance it grants BOOK OF POWER instead (unique cutscene with particles and special text).
  • Potion of Destruction — Crafted at the Crafter in World 2 from 5× NULL FRAGMENT + 5× VOID SHARD + 5× THE UNSPOKEN + 5× ABSOLUTE ZERO (all from Jia — 8,150+ NULL COINS total). Extremely hard to obtain. When used in World 2, it guarantees the Void Queen aura (enemy of the Supreme King) with a full cutscene. Cannot be used in World 1.

Strategy Tips

  • Buy cheap potions across multiple shop rotations and stockpile
  • Use All before your next roll for maximum luck
  • Benny's Bargain Brew is the best luck-per-coin deal at ~1.9 luck/coin
  • The Legendary Elixir is rare but very powerful when it appears
