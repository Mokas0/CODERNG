How Rarity Works

Items are log-spaced. Each item's weight = 1 ÷ rarity. The probability of rolling an item = its weight ÷ total weight of all items in the pool. Lower rarity number = more common (e.g., 1/2 is far more common than 1/1000).

Precision Note

JavaScript's Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER is 9,007,199,254,740,991 (about 9.007×1015). All mythic weights must stay below this ceiling for accurate math. The highest rollable mythic — LAST KNOWN AURA (ID 9072) — has rarity 9×1015, just under the limit.
