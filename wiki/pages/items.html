How Rarity Works

Items are log-spaced. Each item's weight = 1 ÷ rarity. The probability of rolling an item = its weight ÷ total weight of all items in the pool. Lower rarity number = more common (e.g., 1/2 is far more common than 1/1000).

Precision Note

JavaScript's Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER is 9,007,199,254,740,991 (about 9.007×1015). All mythic weights must stay below this ceiling for accurate math. The highest rollable mythic — LAST KNOWN AURA (ID 9072) — has rarity 9×1015, just under the limit.

Mutation Auras

15 twisted variants of existing Mythic auras. They share the same name as the original but have a different visual subtheme (inverted colors, altered fonts) and unique flavor text. Mutations are 3× rarer than their originals and are tagged with a ⟁ MUTATED badge.

IDs: 9100–9114. Rarity range: 3T – 300T.

THE SUPREME KING

ID 9999 — the only UNOBTAINABLE-tier aura. Cannot be rolled normally. Summoned exclusively by using the Supreme Luck Potion (1/10,000 per use). Displayed with gold text (#ffd700) in the Cinzel font, with multi-layered fire and gold text shadows. Features a ~15-second unskippable 6-stage cutscene. History shows with a golden-to-fire pulsing gradient and a "♔ UNOBTAINABLE" badge.

♔ UNOBTAINABLE ♔

THE SUPREME KING stands apart from every other aura tier. It cannot be unlocked through gameplay conditions or rolled from the pool — the only path is the Supreme Luck Potion, which itself has only a 1/100 chance to appear in Benny's shop.
