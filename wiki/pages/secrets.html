Secret Auras

Hidden auras that can't be rolled normally.

How to Get Them

  • Secret auras are triggered ONLY by using the Ultraluck Potion (Benny exclusive, 5,000 coins)
  • Each use has a 1/5,000,000 chance to spawn a secret aura
  • If triggered, one of the 10 secret auras is randomly selected (so each individual aura is effectively 1/50,000,000)
  • Triggers an unskippable cutscene (10 second duration, 8 second minimum view). Each secret aura now has its own unique multi-stage story cutscene, similar to Elder auras.
  • Luck is NOT factored into the secret aura spawn chance — it is always a flat 1 in 5,000,000
  • Rarity displays as "SECRET" (rarity value is 0)

Important

Secret auras cannot be salvaged, traded, or lost. They are permanent trophies.

Supreme Luck Potion — Different Mechanic

Benny also sells the Supreme Luck Potion (1/100 chance to appear per visit, 150,000 coins). While it grants a massive +30,000,000 luck bonus, it has its own separate special trigger: a 1/1,000 chance per use to summon THE SUPREME KING, an UNOBTAINABLE-tier aura (ID 9999). This is entirely distinct from the Ultraluck Potion's secret aura mechanic — the two triggers are independent.
