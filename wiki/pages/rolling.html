Rolling & Luck

How the weighted random system and luck compression determine what you roll.

The Weighted Random Algorithm

Each roll in CODERNG uses a weighted random system where every item has a base weight derived from its rarity:

  • Base weight = 1 ÷ rarity (so weight = 1/rarity)
  • On each roll, luck is compressed: effectiveMult = luck0.4
  • Each item's adjusted weight = weight(1/effectiveMult)
  • Higher effectiveMult flattens weights, making rare items more likely
  • Random selection is made from the adjusted weight pool

Beyond its massive luck bonus, the Supreme Luck Potion has a flat 1/10,000 chance per use to summon THE SUPREME KING — an UNOBTAINABLE-tier aura. This chance is not affected by your luck stat.

How Luck Works

  • Total luck = potion luck (temporary) + gear bonus (permanent)
  • Luck resets to 1× after every roll — potions are consumed on use
  • Gear bonus persists and stacks with any potion luck
  • Buy Luck button: +5 luck for 50 × currentMult coins
Why Luck Compression?

Without compression, extremely high luck would make every item's adjusted weight approach equality. At that point, rarity would cease to matter — a 1/2 item and a 1/9 quadrillion item would have nearly the same chance. The luck^0.4 compression ensures that luck boosts your odds meaningfully without completely flattening the rarity curve. Rare items become more attainable, but they still stay rarer than common ones.
